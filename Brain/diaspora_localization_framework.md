# TVA Diaspora & West African Localization Framework

## Operational Adaptation Guidelines
TVA 2.0 adapts international online schooling conventions for Nigerian and Diaspora families through five specific localization pillars:

1. **Multi-Currency Pricing & Settlement**
   - Local NGN payments via Paystack / Flutterwave for Nigerian residents.
   - International GBP/USD/EUR settlement via Stripe / Wise for Diaspora parents.

2. **Dual Timezone Schedule Engine**
   - Synchronous live lessons aligned with WAT (West Africa Time) and GMT/BST.
   - Asynchronous recorded lesson archives for US/Canada (EST/PST) timezones.

3. **Hybrid British-Nigerian Curriculum Bridge**
   - Core: British National Curriculum (Pearson Edexcel / Cambridge iGCSE).
   - Elective: Nigerian Cultural Studies, History, and optional WAEC/NECO preparation modules.

4. **Low-Bandwidth & Data Resilience**
   - Adaptive video resolution for live Meet sessions.
   - Downloadable offline learning packs and audio-only backup streams for unreliable connectivity.

5. **Parent Trust Telemetry**
   - Automated WhatsApp / SMS attendance alerts for parents.
   - Weekly progress digests delivered directly to parent portals.
