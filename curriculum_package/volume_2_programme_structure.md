# Volume 2: Programme Structure

This document defines the requirements, age boundaries, curriculum pathways, and completion parameters for every educational program offered by Tiptop Virtual Academy.

## 1. Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS)
- **Age Range**: 3 - 5 years.
- **Entry Requirements**: Play-based child assessment, parent interview.
- **Curriculum Coverage**: Communication and Language, Physical Development, Personal, Social and Emotional Development, Literacy, Mathematics, Understanding the World, Expressive Arts and Design.
- **Learning Pathway**: Play-centric, game-based learning tasks, visual activities.
- **Completion Criteria**: Observational milestone compilation by certified educators.

---

## 2. Primary Years (Key Stage 1 & 2)
- **Age Range**: 5 - 11 years.
- **Entry Requirements**: Basic literacy/numeracy evaluation.
- **Curriculum Coverage**: English, Mathematics, Science, Computing, History, Geography, Art & Design.
- **Learning Pathway**: Daily live interactive sessions, cohort activities, guided homework.
- **Completion Criteria**: Submission of termly portfolios, passing End of Key Stage 2 assessments.

---

## 3. Secondary Years (Key Stage 3 & 4 / IGCSE)
- **Age Range**: 11 - 16 years.
- **Entry Requirements**: Academic record review, placement testing.
- **Curriculum Coverage**: Specialized subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), English Literature, Mathematics, Computing, Humanities.
- **Learning Pathway**: Lecture sessions, laboratory simulations, independent research modules.
- **Completion Criteria**: Successful completion of core assignments; preparation for Cambridge IGCSE examinations.

---

## 4. One-to-One Tuition
- **Age Range**: All ages (3 - 18 years).
- **Entry Requirements**: Target objective evaluation.
- **Curriculum Coverage**: Targeted support in specific subjects (Maths, English, Science).
- **Learning Pathway**: Highly customized, direct lesson plans tailored to individual needs.
- **Completion Criteria**: Achievement of specific student goals; positive educator review.

---

## 5. Holiday School & After School Support
- **Age Range**: 5 - 16 years.
- **Entry Requirements**: Open enrollment.
- **Curriculum Coverage**: Creative writing, STEM workshops, project challenges.
- **Learning Pathway**: Project-based team cohorts, collaborative workshops, practical assignments.
- **Completion Criteria**: Completion of a hands-on project presentation.
