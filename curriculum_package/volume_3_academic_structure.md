# Volume 3: Academic Structure

This document establishes the delivery structure of the British Curriculum Package, defining the timeline hierarchy from the academic year down to individual learning activities.

## 1. Timeline Hierarchy Schema

```
[Academic Year] (36 Weeks Total)
       │
       ├─► [Term 1: Autumn] (12 Weeks)
       ├─► [Term 2: Spring] (12 Weeks)
       └─► [Term 3: Summer] (12 Weeks)
                 │
                 └─► [Weekly Increments] (e.g. Week 4)
                           │
                           └─► [Subject Modules] (e.g. Mathematics)
                                     │
                                     └─► [Units] (e.g. Fractions & Decimals)
                                               │
                                               └─► [Lessons] (e.g. Adding Fractions)
                                                         │
                                                         ├─► [Learning Objectives] (e.g. Add unlike denominators)
                                                         ├─► [Activities] (e.g. Interactive math models)
                                                         └─► [Assessments] (e.g. Formative quiz check-in)
```

---

## 2. Hierarchy Example: Primary Year 5 Mathematics

The following example demonstrates how a specific curriculum node is decomposed under this structure:

### 2.1 Academic Year: Year 5 (Primary)
- **Term**: Term 1 (Autumn), Weeks 1–12.
- **Weekly Slice**: Week 6.
- **Subject**: Mathematics.

---

### 2.2 Unit: Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages (Unit Ref: `Y5-M-U03`)
- **Unit Scope**: Understanding fractions with different denominators, decimal equivalents, and percentage values.
- **Lesson 3**: Adding Fractions with Different Denominators (Lesson Ref: `Y5-M-U03-L03`).

---

### 2.3 Lesson Components (`Y5-M-U03-L03`)

#### Learning Objectives:
- **Primary Objective**: Identify, write, and calculate equivalent fractions for a given fraction.
- **Secondary Objective**: Add fractions where one denominator is a multiple of the other.

#### Activities:
- **Interactive Component**: Digital visual fraction bars showing equivalent denominators.
- **Collaboration Activity**: Group whiteboard exercise matching equivalent fractions.

#### Assessments:
- **Formative Assessment**: A 3-question exit ticket checking understanding of denominator conversion.
- **AI Companion Prompt**: Socratic prompt helping students find common denominators.
