# Volume 4: Subject Architecture

This document defines the scope, learning outcomes, resource recommendations, and evaluation strategies for the primary academic subjects offered at Tiptop Virtual Academy.

## 1. Subject: Mathematics

### 1.1 Scope
Covers number theory, calculations, geometry, statistics, algebra, ratio, proportion, and measurement structures based on the National Curriculum for England.

### 1.2 Learning Outcomes
- **Fluency**: Recall mathematical facts and perform calculations quickly and accurately.
- **Reasoning**: Formulate mathematical arguments and identify relationships between concepts.
- **Problem Solving**: Apply mathematical concepts to solve unfamiliar, real-world problems.

### 1.3 Progression & Dependencies
- **Pre-requisite**: Basic arithmetic operations must be mastered before introducing fractions.
- **Downstream**: Understanding fractions is required for ratios, algebra, and physics equations.

### 1.4 Assessment Strategy
- **Continuous**: Weekly interactive quizzes.
- **Practical**: Project tasks (e.g. geometric design modeling).
- **Summative**: Termly exams and end-of-year progression tests.

## 2. Subject: English Language & Literature

### 2.1 Scope
Covers reading comprehension, vocabulary enrichment, spelling, grammar, creative writing, analytical reading, and verbal communication skills.

### 2.2 Learning Outcomes
- **Analytical Reading**: Analyze text structure, identify literary devices, and evaluate character motivation.
- **Writing**: Produce clear, structured text adapted to different audiences and styles.
- **Speaking & Listening**: Present arguments clearly and respond constructively in group discussions.

### 2.3 Progression & Dependencies
- **Pre-requisite**: Sentence structure mastery.
- **Downstream**: Analytical writing supports history, geography, and science reporting.

### 2.4 Assessment Strategy
- **Continuous**: Weekly reading logs and spelling checks.
- **Creative**: Peer-reviewed creative writing assignments.
- **Summative**: Analytical essays and termly reading assessments.

---

## 3. Subject: Science

### 3.1 Scope
Covers Biology (living things, ecosystems, human body), Chemistry (states of matter, chemical changes), and Physics (forces, electricity, space science).

### 3.2 Learning Outcomes
- **Scientific Method**: Formulate hypotheses, design experiments, and record results.
- **Analysis**: Interpret charts, evaluate scientific data, and draw logical conclusions.
- **Application**: Connect scientific concepts to real-world applications (e.g., green energy).

### 3.3 Progression & Dependencies
- **Pre-requisite**: Base mathematical calculations (for physics equations).
- **Downstream**: Prepares students for advanced science examinations (IGCSE/A-Levels).

### 3.4 Assessment Strategy
- **Continuous**: Digital lab report submissions.
- **Collaborative**: Group research projects.
- **Summative**: Termly tests evaluating conceptual understanding.
