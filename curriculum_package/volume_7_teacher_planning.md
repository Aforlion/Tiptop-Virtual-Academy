# Volume 7: Teacher Planning Framework

This document defines the structured lesson planning templates, differentiation guidelines, and resource allocation systems for educators at Tiptop Virtual Academy.

## 1. Planning Cycle Layers

```
[Long-Term Plan (LTP)] (Annual Curriculum Schemes)
         │
         ▼
[Medium-Term Plan (MTP)] (Termly Unit Breakdowns)
         │
         ▼
[Weekly Lesson Schemes] (Weekly Sequence & Objectives)
         │
         ▼
[Daily Lesson Plans] (Daily Lesson Delivery & Activities)
```

- **Long-Term Plan**: Outlines high-level curriculum coverage across three terms (Autumn, Spring, Summer).
- **Medium-Term Plan**: Focuses on specific units (e.g. Fractions), outlining key objectives and resource needs for a 4-to-6 week period.
- **Weekly Lesson Schemes**: Outlines the sequence of daily lessons, target learning objectives, vocabulary terms, and individual homework tasks.
- **Daily Lesson Plans**: Specific lesson plans outlining session intros, student activities, plenary reviews, and differentiation actions.

---

## 2. Daily Lesson Plan Template

Every daily lesson plan compiled by teachers (or drafted by the AI Teaching Partner) must match this structure:

```markdown
# Lesson Plan: [Title]
- **Date/Week**: Term [T], Week [W], Day [D]
- **Subject/Year**: [Subject] / [Year]
- **Focus Objective**: [Specific objective, e.g. Convert decimals to fractions]

## 1. Lesson Sequence
- **Starter (10 mins)**: High-interest warm-up task to review prior knowledge.
- **Direct Instruction (15 mins)**: Explaining the main concept with visual aids.
- **Independent Practice (25 mins)**: Students complete graded activities.
- **Plenary (10 mins)**: Exit ticket and student self-assessment.

## 2. Differentiation Actions
- **Support Group (SEN/Lower Ability)**: Provide simplified visual guides and scaffolded activities.
- **Core Group (On-track)**: Standard independent activities.
- **Extension Group (Higher Ability)**: Challenging multi-step problem-solving tasks.

## 3. Resources Required
- [List links, e.g. Whiteboard slides, worksheets, digital models]
```

---

## 3. Differentiation Guidelines

- **Play-Based (Early Years)**: Activities adapt based on engagement observations.
- **Structured Scaffolding (Key Stage 1 & 2)**: Core concepts are introduced with visual support, gradually moving to independent calculations.
- **Advanced Extensions (Key Stage 3 & 4)**: Students who finish early are assigned open-ended problem-solving tasks rather than extra practice questions.
